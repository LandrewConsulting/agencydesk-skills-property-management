# Glossary

These entries are general documentary labels to help read a document, not legal advice or a determination of terms, coverage, or compliance.

The status definitions and permitted output labels in SKILL.md take precedence over this glossary. Do not introduce glossary labels that are not authorized by the skill's output instructions.

- **Not found in supplied documents**: The information is absent from all supplied documents.
- **Not listed on [named source]**: An item appears on one supplied document and does not appear on the named comparison document.
- **Unclear — unreadable or ambiguous**: The text cannot be reliably read or interpreted.
- **Verification required**: Some evidence is shown, but the underlying document or source record must be confirmed by a qualified person. For a single-source readable item, Verification required means qualified review is still needed and does not imply an error or discrepancy.
- **Documented match**: The information shown matches the stated requirement or description based only on the supplied documents.
- **Documented difference**: The information shown differs from the stated requirement or the other supplied document based only on the supplied documents.

Always quote source text exactly. Never invent missing information.
